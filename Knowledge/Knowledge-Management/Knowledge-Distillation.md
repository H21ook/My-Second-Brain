# Knowledge Distillation

## Summary

Knowledge Distillation is the process of transforming raw information into reusable knowledge.

Its purpose is to remove unnecessary detail while preserving understanding, decisions, principles, and lessons that create long-term value.

Distillation is the bridge between temporary information and permanent knowledge.

---

## Problem

Raw information is expensive to maintain.

AI conversations, articles, documentation, and meeting notes often contain useful insights, but they also contain repetition, temporary context, and irrelevant detail.

Without distillation, a knowledge base becomes difficult to search, maintain, and reuse.

---

## When to Use

Use Knowledge Distillation when information comes from:

- AI conversations
- documentation
- books
- videos
- research
- meetings
- debugging
- personal experience

Distill only when the result is likely to create future value.

---

## When Not to Use

Do not distill information that has no expected future value.

Temporary tasks, casual conversations, and low-value information should be discarded instead of preserved.

Do not use distillation as a way to store everything in a cleaner format.

---

## Core Concepts

Knowledge Distillation separates:

```text
Information
  -> Understanding
  -> Knowledge
  -> Reusable Asset
```

The objective is not to remember everything.

The objective is to preserve only what improves future thinking.

---

## How It Works

1. Capture raw information.
2. Evaluate whether it has future value.
3. Remove unnecessary context.
4. Extract reusable concepts.
5. Organize the knowledge.
6. Connect it to related notes.
7. Review and improve it over time.

---

## Best Practices

- Preserve principles instead of conversations.
- Write in your own words.
- Focus on reusable knowledge.
- Link related notes.
- Prefer one clear concept per note.
- Separate project-specific details from general lessons.
- Improve notes after reuse exposes gaps.

---

## Common Mistakes

- Saving entire AI conversations.
- Copying documentation without understanding.
- Mixing multiple concepts in one note.
- Keeping project-specific details in general knowledge.
- Creating duplicate notes instead of improving existing ones.
- Distilling too early before the useful lesson is clear.

---

## Trade-offs

Advantages:

- smaller and higher-quality knowledge base
- better AI context
- faster retrieval
- easier maintenance
- less duplication

Disadvantages:

- requires time to summarize
- some context is intentionally discarded
- requires judgment about future value

---

## Examples

Poor:

```text
ChatGPT conversation
  -> Stored directly
```

Better:

```text
ChatGPT conversation
  -> Extract three reusable ideas
  -> Create or update focused Knowledge Notes
  -> Link related notes
```

## Personal Insights

Every AI conversation should end with one question:

> What reusable knowledge did I gain?

If the answer is nothing, do not create a note.

If the answer is a principle, create or update a Knowledge Note.

If the answer is a decision, create a Decision Note.
