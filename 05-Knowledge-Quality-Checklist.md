# Knowledge Quality Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist ensures that every permanent knowledge note meets the quality standards of this Second Brain.

It is not intended to judge how much information a note contains.

Instead, it evaluates whether the note is clear, reusable, maintainable, and valuable for both humans and AI.

A note does not need to satisfy every item perfectly, but it should meet the majority before becoming permanent knowledge.

---

# Content Quality

## Clarity

-  The title clearly describes the topic.
    
-  The note focuses on a single concept.
    
-  The main idea can be understood within a few minutes.
    
-  The language is clear and unambiguous.
    

## Understanding

-  The note explains **what** it is.
    
-  The note explains **why** it matters.
    
-  The note explains **when** it should be used.
    
-  The note explains important trade-offs or limitations.
    
-  The note avoids unnecessary details.
    

## Original Thinking

-  The content is written in my own words.
    
-  The note reflects understanding instead of copied information.
    
-  Key insights or lessons are included.
    

---

# Reusability

-  The knowledge is likely to be useful again.
    
-  The note can be reused across multiple projects.
    
-  The content is evergreen or has long-term value.
    
-  The note reduces future research effort.
    
-  The note reduces future AI context and token usage.
    

---

# Structure

-  The note follows the standard note structure.
    
-  Headings are organized logically.
    
-  Examples are included where appropriate.
    
-  Related notes are linked.
    
-  Metadata and tags are complete if required.
    

---

# AI Readability

-  AI can understand the note without additional context.
    
-  Important terms are clearly defined.
    
-  Abbreviations are explained when necessary.
    
-  The note contains enough context to answer future questions.
    

---

# Maintenance

-  The information is still accurate.
    
-  No duplicate note already exists.
    
-  Outdated information has been removed.
    
-  The note has been reviewed after significant learning.
    

---

# Final Questions

Before marking this note as permanent knowledge, ask:

-  Will I probably need this again?
    
-  Would future me understand this without additional explanation?
    
-  Would AI be able to use this note effectively?
    
-  Does this note save future time?
    
-  If I deleted this note today, would I regret losing it in one year?
    

---

# Quality Levels

## Excellent

- Nearly every checklist item is satisfied.
    
- The note is reusable, well structured, and continuously maintainable.
    

## Good

- Most important items are satisfied.
    
- Minor improvements can be made later.
    

## Draft

- Valuable information exists but still requires refinement.
    

## Temporary

- Raw information.
    
- Should not become permanent knowledge until improved.
    

---

# Principle

Quality is more important than quantity.

Every permanent note should increase the long-term value of the Second Brain.

If a note cannot clearly justify its existence, it should be improved, archived, or removed.
